# Privacy policy — PaperCript

*Last updated: 2 November 2026*

> **Notice.** This privacy policy is under legal review. The revised version will replace this text once
> the review is complete. Until then, this version applies.

PaperCript, by javimentallab, collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no
analytics and no crash reporting, and it removes the Internet permission, so it cannot
send anything anywhere.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — used only when you scan a card's QR code to decrypt it. The frames are
  analysed on the device and are never saved or sent.

## What is stored on the device

Nothing. Passwords, the master password and the cards you build are kept in memory only
and are gone when you close the app. App backup is disabled.

## Printing

When you print a sheet, it is handed to Android's print system and to the print service
you choose (for example your printer's plug-in or "Save as PDF"). What happens to the
sheet from there depends on that service.

## Clipboard

If you tap **Copy**, the value goes to the Android clipboard. PaperCript clears it after a
short time or when you come back to the app, because Android does not let apps touch the
clipboard from the background. Until then other apps you allow can read it.

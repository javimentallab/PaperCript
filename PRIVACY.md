# Privacy policy — PaperCript

*Last updated: 8 November 2026*

*Versión en español: [PRIVACY.es.md](PRIVACY.es.md)*

## 1. Data controller

The PaperCript application (the "App") is published on Google Play by [REDACTED], with address at [REDACTED] (the "Provider"), under the developer name javimentallab. The Provider is the controller of the personal data referred to in this policy, and the User may contact the Provider at [REDACTED].

## 2. No processing of personal data in the App

The App does not collect or process any personal data of the User. The App has no user accounts, advertising, analytics tools or crash reporting systems, and it lacks the Internet access permission, so it cannot transmit data to third parties. The only personal data processed by the Provider are those relating to the purchase of the App, described in section 7.

## 3. Permissions

- **Camera:** used solely to scan the QR code of a card in order to decrypt it. Images are analysed on the device itself, are neither stored nor transmitted, and no person has access to them.

## 4. Data stored on the device

The App stores no data on the device. Passwords, the master password and the cards prepared by the User are kept only in the device's memory and are deleted when the App is closed. The App's backup is disabled and will not be enabled.

## 5. Printing

When the User prints a sheet, it is handed over to the Android print system and to the print service chosen by the User, for example the printer's plug-in or "Save as PDF". Any further processing of the sheet depends on that service.

## 6. Clipboard

If the User uses the Copy function, the value is transferred to the Android clipboard. The App clears it after a short period or when the User returns to the App, since Android does not allow applications to access the clipboard in the background. Until then, other applications authorised by the User can access it.

## 7. Purchase data

The App is purchased through Google Play, which handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. The Provider does not receive the User's payment details. For each order, Google provides the Provider with limited data, such as the order number, the date, the amount and the buyer's country.

As required by Article 13 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 (General Data Protection Regulation), the following information is provided:

- **Purpose:** to manage orders and refunds and to comply with the Provider's accounting and tax obligations.
- **Legal basis:** the performance of the purchase contract (Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation) and compliance with the Provider's legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).
- **Retention period:** the period required by tax law; as a general rule, four years (Article 66 of the Spanish General Taxation Act). After that period, the data are deleted.
- **Recipients:** the data are not disclosed to third parties, except where required by law.

## 8. User's rights

The User may exercise the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and portability, under Articles 15 to 22 of the General Data Protection Regulation, by contacting the Provider at [REDACTED]. The User may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).
